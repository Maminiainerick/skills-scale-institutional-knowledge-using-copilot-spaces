# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define the testing strategy, validate quality outcomes, and ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release. They work closely with Developers and Product Managers to reduce risk and increase confidence in delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy, test plans, and release quality gates
- Create and maintain automated and manual test coverage
- Validate acceptance criteria against user and business requirements
- Coordinate smoke tests, regression coverage, and defect triage
- Report quality trends, risks, and blockers to the project team
- Partner with Developers on testability, automation, and defect resolution

### Goals
- Improve product quality and reduce production defects
- Increase confidence in releases through evidence-based validation
- Align quality checks with customer value and acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- PR review feedback on test coverage and edge cases
- Weekly quality status and defect reviews
- Release readiness checkpoints with PMs and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to ensure features are testable and regressions are caught early
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release quality expectations
- Supports Project Managers by flagging delivery risk and release readiness

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide system design, technical standards, and engineering decisions. They help the team balance speed, maintainability, and long-term scalability while coordinating with delivery and product roles.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture and technical designs for quality and alignment
- Set technical direction, standards, and reusable patterns
- Identify technical debt and recommend mitigation strategies
- Support major design decisions, trade-offs, and technical risk management
- Mentor Developers and guide implementation consistency
- Coordinate with QA and DevOps on performance, reliability, and operational considerations

### Goals
- Maintain a scalable, maintainable, and resilient technical foundation
- Reduce avoidable technical debt and delivery bottlenecks
- Improve engineering consistency across the project

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architecture decision records
- Technical syncs with engineering leads and stakeholders
- Code review feedback focused on maintainability and system trade-offs
- Planning discussions around dependencies, integrations, and non-functional requirements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers on technical implementation and design alignment
- Advises Product Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and trade-offs
- Supports Project Managers by identifying delivery risks related to architecture and dependencies

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, funding support, and strategic alignment. They help ensure the project remains aligned with organizational goals and decision-making priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Champion the business case and define strategic priorities
- Approve scope, milestones, and major decisions when needed
- Provide executive updates and unblock cross-functional dependencies
- Align project outcomes with business goals and customer needs
- Support prioritization and trade-off decisions across initiatives

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers meaningful business value
- Maintain executive alignment and sponsorship throughout delivery
- Support successful outcomes and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Decision reviews and escalation pathways
- Business briefings and executive summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to clarify business objectives and value metrics
- Collaborates with Project Managers on prioritization, funding, and timeline decisions
- Receives delivery updates from the project team and provides strategic direction when needed

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help the team work effectively by facilitating delivery rhythms, removing blockers, and improving collaboration. They support continuous improvement without owning the product direction itself.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, retrospectives, and reviews
- Help the team identify blockers, dependencies, and workflow issues
- Coach teams on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Improve team collaboration, clarity, and delivery flow
- Support adoption of working agreements and role clarity

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness, predictability, and delivery consistency
- Reduce friction in planning, execution, and review cycles
- Help teams learn and adapt quickly

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation and team coaching
- Retrospective discussions and action tracking
- Refinement sessions focused on workflow and impediments

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Developers and Project Managers by improving team flow and problem-solving
- Works with Product Managers to ensure backlog refinement and sprint readiness
- Helps stakeholders understand delivery cadence and team health without replacing project ownership

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure projects meet required security, privacy, and regulatory expectations. They help teams identify risk early and establish controls that protect users, data, and the business.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements for security, privacy, and compliance impact
- Help define controls, standards, and review checkpoints
- Assess risks and identify mitigation needs for projects and releases
- Partner with engineering and operations on secure design and deployment practices
- Support incidents, policy exceptions, and required reporting

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Ensure release readiness meets required controls and standards
- Build trust with users, stakeholders, and compliance audiences

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and policy discussions
- Risk assessment updates during planning and delivery
- Release readiness and incident coordination when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads on secure design and implementation patterns
- Collaborates with Project Managers to assess risk and sequencing of work
- Provides guidance to Product Managers and Sponsors on compliance constraints and risk trade-offs

---

## Operations / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations and DevOps Engineers support delivery, deployment, monitoring, and environment reliability. They help teams ship safely and maintain service health across development, staging, and production.

### Responsibilities
- Manage environments, deployment pipelines, and release automation
- Configure monitoring, alerts, and observability for production systems
- Support incident response and rollback planning when needed
- Collaborate with engineering teams on operational readiness and scalability
- Improve deployment safety, repeatability, and recovery workflows

### Goals
- Improve deployment reliability and service stability
- Reduce operational friction for delivery teams
- Strengthen observability and response readiness

### Typical Communication
- Deployment readiness reviews and release coordination
- Operational incident updates and postmortems
- DevOps and infrastructure planning with engineering leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers and Technical Leads to support secure and reliable releases
- Coordinates with QA/Testing Leads on smoke tests and release validation
- Supports Project Managers and Sponsors with deployment risk visibility and production readiness information

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles should be treated as complementary, with accountability distributed across the project while preserving clear ownership for delivery, decisions, and risk.
